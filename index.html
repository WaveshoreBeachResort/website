About Waveshore Beach Resort

At Waveshore Beach Resort, we believe that a perfect getaway combines the warmth of home with the magic of the sea. Located just steps away from one of the region's most popular and breathtaking beaches, our resort offers a peaceful, refreshing escape for families, couples, and adventurers.

We provide a premium homestay experience with clean, cozy rooms designed for comfort and relaxation. Our in-house kitchen serves quality, fresh, and delicious meals, giving every guest a blend of local flavors and homely warmth.

For guests who seek a more adventurous and nature-connected stay, we also offer tent camping. Fall asleep under the stars, wake up to the sound of waves, and enjoy the raw beauty of coastal life.

Frequently Asked Questions

You can book a camping experience directly through booking option in each of the listed campsites. There is a minimum number of pax required for bookings & the minimum number differs from campsite to campsite. If you are a solo traveller looking to camp you can join any of the camping events scheduled under upcoming events section. There are also camps that accept 1 pax.

The campsite has common, neat western toilets with running water. There are 9 restrooms in total, and cabins for changing clothes are also available to ensure privacy and convenience.

Camp menu differs from campsite to campsite with most of them serving Veg and Non-veg food.
